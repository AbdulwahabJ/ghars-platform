MODE: Power

Implement SAFE HISTORICAL / OPENING FINANCE support for Ghars Legacy Import.

IMPORTANT CONTEXT:

Ghars must support TWO types of legacy patients:

1. Historical completed patients
   - treatment happened in the past
   - may already be financially settled

2. Historical but STILL ACTIVE patients
   - treatment started in the past
   - implants/procedures may have occurred in 2024/2025
   - treatment may still be ongoing today
   - prosthetic work may still be pending
   - patient may still have an outstanding balance
   - future payments/follow-ups/prosthetic events will continue normally inside Ghars

This distinction is CRITICAL.

Production contains live customer data.

DO NOT:
- invent payment dates
- invent payment methods
- treat unknown amounts as zero
- mark old patients as completed simply because they are historical
- create fake prosthetic events
- overwrite existing Production finance
- publish automatically

Use Development/test data only.

==================================================

Never default historical prosthetic event_date to import date or today.
