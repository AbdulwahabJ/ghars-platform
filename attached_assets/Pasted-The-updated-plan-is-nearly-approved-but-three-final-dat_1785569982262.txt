- implant_cases.base_treatment_amount
- case_charges
- case_discounts
- valid non-voided payments

Final Case Total =
Base Treatment Amount
+ Sum of Charges
- Sum of Discounts

Paid Amount =
Sum of valid non-voided Payments

Outstanding Balance =
Final Case Total - Paid Amount

Payment Percentage =
Paid Amount / Final Case Total, with safe zero-total handling
