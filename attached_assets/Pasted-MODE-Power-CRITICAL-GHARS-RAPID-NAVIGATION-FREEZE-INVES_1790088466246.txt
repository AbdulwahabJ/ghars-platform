MODE: Power

CRITICAL GHARS RAPID-NAVIGATION FREEZE INVESTIGATION AND ROOT FIX.

REAL REPRODUCTION:

The app works normally during slow/normal usage.

BUT if the user rapidly:

- clicks between Dashboard / Patients / Statistics / Settings
- switches Settings tabs quickly
- clicks multiple navigation items repeatedly
- rapidly expands/collapses records
- performs many clicks one after another

the ENTIRE application eventually freezes and becomes unresponsive.

This is highly reproducible under rapid interaction.

This is now a RELEASE-BLOCKING stability issue.

DO NOT add features.
DO NOT redesign.
DO NOT publish.
DO NOT solve only by adding debounce/throttle.
Find and fix the actual concurrency/render/request problem.
