==================================================
FOLLOW-UP RULES
==================================================

Use structured rules, not free-text interpretation.

- Today’s scheduled incomplete follow-ups are considered today’s appointments.
- Past incomplete follow-ups are overdue.
- Follow-ups with requires_contact = true and a due contact date are contact tasks.
- Cases with status جاهز للتركيب are ready-for-treatment cases.
- Completed and cancelled follow-ups must not appear as overdue.
- Postponing a follow-up must preserve the original history and record the new date.
- Follow-up actions must be blocked when the patient file is archived.

==================================================

- delivered,
- read,

Create the approved templates:
