The Phase 1 plan is approved subject to the following final implementation requirements.

These are clarifications within the approved scope, not new features.

1. USER-LEVEL PERMISSION OVERRIDES

The database foundation must support the two previously approved configurable permissions:

- Doctor financial visibility.
- Assistant payment-recording permission.

Add a simple maintainable permission model now, such as explicit user-level boolean permission overrides or an equivalent minimal structure.

Do not build the full user-management UI in Phase 1; that remains Phase 6.

Backend authorization must later be able to distinguish:

- a DOCTOR who may view financial summaries,
- a DOCTOR who may not,
- an ASSISTANT who may record payments,
- an ASSISTANT who may not.

Do not rely only on the role name for these two permissions.

2. USER PREFERENCES UNIQUENESS

user_preferences must have exactly one row per user.

Enforce this using:

UNIQUE(user_id)

Do not use an “active preference row” pattern and do not allow multiple preference records for the same user.

3. REPLIT PROXY AND SECURE SESSION HANDLING

Because the application runs behind the Replit reverse proxy, configure Express proxy handling correctly.

Use an appropriately limited trust-proxy configuration such as:

app.set("trust proxy", 1)

Ensure:

- secure session cookies work in production,
- rate limiting resolves the client address safely,
- arbitrary forwarded headers are not blindly trusted,
- development remains usable over local HTTP,
- production cookies are HTTP-only, Secure, SameSite, and scoped safely.

Do not disable secure-cookie behavior merely to make development easier.

4. CSRF AND SAME-ORIGIN PROTECTION

SameSite cookies alone are not the entire CSRF strategy.

For state-changing requests, implement a clear same-origin protection strategy using either:

- validated Origin and Host checks appropriate for the deployment domain,
- or a compatible CSRF-token mechanism.

Do not apply CSRF protection to the health endpoint.

Document the chosen approach in the Phase 1 completion report and test that legitimate frontend requests work while cross-origin mutation requests are rejected.

5. CLINIC LOGO ASSET

Copy the attached clinic logo into the application using a safe simple internal filename such as:

clinic-logo.png

Do not keep the long Arabic upload filename as the runtime asset path.

Preserve image quality and aspect ratio.
Do not redraw or modify the clinic logo.
