FINANCIAL SOURCE OF TRUTH

Use only:

- implant_cases.base_treatment_amount
- case_charges
- case_discounts
- valid non-voided payments

Calculate dynamically:

Final Case Total =
Base Treatment Amount
+ Sum of Charges
- Sum of Discounts

Paid Amount =
Sum of valid non-voided Payments

Outstanding Balance =
Final Case Total - Paid Amount

Collected Revenue for a period =
Sum of valid payments whose payment_date is within the selected period

Do not calculate collected revenue from procedure date or case creation date.

Do not store mutable derived totals.

Do not allow users to manually edit:

- paid amount,
- outstanding balance,
- final total,
- or payment percentage.

MONEY TYPES
