5. IMAGE FIT — VERY IMPORTANT
=============================

The image MUST fit professionally.

Current bad cropping must be fixed.

Use:

object-fit: cover

but choose an intelligent portrait-oriented object position.

Default recommendation:

object-position: center 20%

or another top-biased position that prioritizes:

1. face
2. shoulders
3. upper body

Do not center-crop blindly if it cuts the user's head.

Never stretch or distort the image.

Never use:

object-fit: fill

The source image aspect ratio must always be preserved.

If the uploaded image is unusually wide or tall, the design must still degrade gracefully.
